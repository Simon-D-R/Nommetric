Nommetric

Stub. Open the console.

On load this runs a store harness against the reserved restaurant __harness__: nine steps covering load, an id-keyed write, a name-keyed write, resolution, the name → id promotion, its no-op re-run, and the empty-record removal. It cleans up after itself.

Also available in the console: dump_all() prints every stored key, run_harness(true) re-runs the sequence but stops before the cleanup so a second tab can observe the record via onChanged, and wipe_harness() removes the __harness__ keys.
